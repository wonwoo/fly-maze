Fly Maze

A MaleCNS connectome-derived circuit plays a maze

A fixed 160-cell circuit measured in the MaleCNS v1.0 connectome receives twenty maze observations on gustatory, visual and proprioceptive cells. Thirty-two descending cells feed a small trained readout that chooses a direction. Only the readout learns. The biological wiring never changes.

    Cells sit at their measured soma coordinates, seen from the front. A cell's brightness and a connection's brightness are its computed activity, so the wiring that is carrying the current decision is what stands out. Only the strongest measured connections are drawn.
